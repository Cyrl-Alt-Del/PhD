On Other Regarding Choices

Taking just one dimension of possible virtues, the set that are ‘other regarding’ (justice, charity, fairness, generosity, etc.). One could assume that, when I think about how to act:
I have a utility function for myself
I have a utility function for others
I have weighting for that utility function for others, based on things like social distance / role expectations e.g.: “family, friend, colleague, stranger, competitor, enemy”
I have a utility function for how much I value this resource I may or may not share (or how much this action may or may not benefit me)
I have a weighting for that utility function for others based on my guess about how much they value a resource I could share or how much they will be benefited / harmed by my action
I have some estimation of a total utility for our interaction, weighted by its likelihood to be repeating
I have some sort of weighting for the utility of our interaction based on some simple ripple effects like “does this benefit / harm anyone I know or any relationship I do value?”
Some of these may just be grouped together as some sort of longterm delta. 
Also, it’s interesting to consider which of these are strategic considerations rather than “what sort of person am I?” type considerations
